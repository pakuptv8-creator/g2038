# Pokémon Battle Strategy Guide (ULTRA MOD VERSION)

## Featured Pets & Counters

### 1. Apophis (ID: 10500501) - The Psychic Slayer
- **Type:** Psychic (Race 5)
- **Stats:** High Speed and Magic Attack.
- **Ideal Skill Build:**
    1. **Psychic Blade (1105010):** 400 Fixed Damage. The ultimate shield breaker.
    2. **Izanagi (1105008):** Speed +40% & Status Immunity. Essential for moving first.
    3. **Psychic Nova (1105004):** Massive damage (x2) if target is paralyzed.
    4. **Steel Skin (1101007):** Preemptive defense buff (+30%) to survive the first hit.

**Tactic against Psynesis (Psychic):**
- Psynesis relies on confusion. Use **Izanagi** on Round 1 to become immune.
- Follow up with **Psychic Blade**. Since fixed damage ignores its high psychic resistance, you will melt its HP faster than it can burst you.

**Tactic against Waspthorn (Special):**
- Waspthorn is slow but hits hard. Use **Psychic Blade** immediately.
- If Waspthorn survives, use **Steel Skin** to mitigate its "Special" type damage bonus.

### 2. Turtlemage (ID: 10201001) - The Tanky Mage
- **Type:** Water (Race 2)
- **Stats:** High HP and Defense.
- **Ideal Skill Build:**
    1. **Ice World (1102014):** 400 Fixed Damage. Your main nuke.
    2. **Cold Wave (1102013):** 250 Fixed Damage + Power Buff. Use to scale mid-fight.
    3. **Ice Mist (1102006):** Preemptive Speed buff (+40%). Compensates for low base speed.
    4. **Stun Pollen (1101006):** 60% Paralyze. Controls the enemy and sets up teammates.

**Tactic against Psynesis (Psychic):**
- **Risky Matchup.** Psynesis deals 1.5x to Water.
- Use **Ice Mist** to try and outspeed, then **Stun Pollen**. If you land the paralyze, Psynesis might lose turns.
- Spam **Ice World**. Do not use normal Water moves as they deal reduced damage to Psychic types.

**Tactic against Waspthorn (Special):**
- Waspthorn deals 1.2x to Water.
- Use **Ice Mist** -> **Ice World**. Since you have high base HP, you can usually trade hits effectively using Fixed Damage.
